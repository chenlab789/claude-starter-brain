# Welcome — how this brain works

This is a seed note. It explains the vault to both you and your AI. You can delete
it once you get the idea.

## What this vault is
`Brain` is your AI's long-term memory, stored as plain Markdown notes you can
read in Obsidian. When your AI learns something, it writes a note here. Over time it
becomes a map of what your AI knows — about its tasks, about you, and about mistakes
it has learned from.

## How the AI uses it
1. Every session it reads [[00_Index]] to recall what it already knows.
2. When it learns something worth keeping, it writes a new note in the right folder.
3. It links related notes with `[[double brackets]]` so they form a graph.
4. It logs any mistakes in `lessons/` so they don't happen twice.

## How YOU use it
- Open this folder as a vault in Obsidian (File → Open folder as vault).
- Browse notes, read what your AI knows, edit anything you want.
- Use the Graph View (left sidebar) to see how notes connect.

## Folders
- `identity/` — who the AI is, who you are
- `knowledge/` — general facts and learned info
- `lessons/` — errors + fixes
- `projects/` — ongoing work
